**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, approximately 15 seconds  
**Visual rule:** Use screen recordings or screenshots captured directly from the real Med101 website/app. Do not use AI-generated UI, stock photos, fake browser mockups, or invented features.

## Shot sequence

| Time | Capture from the real app | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Real Med101 loading/intro screen, captured from the app | **MED101** |

## Capture checklist

- Open the deployed Med101 site and record the actual interface in portrait orientation.
- Use a real test/student account and real available subject/question content.
- Hide or avoid names, email addresses, notifications, private messages, and other personal data.
- Keep taps deliberate; pause briefly on each screen so the text can be read.
- Crop only for vertical framing. Do not replace, redraw, or fabricate any part of the interface.
- Add the short captions above in the video editor, not inside the app UI.
- Use a music track that is licensed for the account and platform where the Reel will be posted.
